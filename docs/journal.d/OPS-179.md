## What happened

The doctor tests picked lines by substring (`lineWith(out, "own")`), and doctor's `template` line prints the checkout's path, so a checkout under a path holding the word failed or falsely passed them (#359). A helper, `subjectLine(out, subject, levels...)`, matches a line by its level and subject COLUMNS. Every absence assert uses it, and `needLine` matches its first two parts as columns whenever the first is a level label. It was shown failing first: TestDoctorOwnOrPluginModelIsNotCheckedForDrift failed from this lane (`.claude/worktrees/fix-359-doctor-own-path`). From a copy at a path holding own, model, drift, jq and FAIL, origin/main's test failed three doctor tests. With this change, every doctor test passes from that copy and from the lane.

## Decisions

- Close the class, not the line (PRAC-0010): round 1 found the same substring match in four more absence asserts, and round 2 found it in the presence lookups. Both are fixed by column matching in the two helpers, so a new assert inherits it.
- Lookups by a fixed text holding a colon or spaces (`fix:`, `gh auth login`) stay as substrings: a path holding them is implausible (the reviewer agreed).
